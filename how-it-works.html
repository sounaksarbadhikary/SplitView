A SHARED MONEY RITUAL

Less chasing.
More knowing.

SplitFlow turns a messy group tab into a simple shared record. Here is the rhythm that keeps every room clear.

01◈

Create one room

Start a room for a trip, flat, project, or recurring group. Give it a name that everyone will recognise.

  • Use one room per shared context.
  • Keep the invite code within the group.
02＋

Log it while it is fresh

Add the expense soon after paying so the details, category, and amount stay accurate.

  • Use a short, specific title.
  • Enter the full amount in rupees.
03↔

Keep the split visible

Your room shows total spending, an equal-share estimate, and the balance created by each payment.

  • Review the activity log before settling up.
  • Talk through unusual or shared costs.
04✓

Settle with context

The balance is a guide for a fair conversation, not a substitute for your group’s agreement.

  • Confirm who was included in a cost.
  • Mark corrections by adding a clear follow-up expense.
