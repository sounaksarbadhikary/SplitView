01

What we collect

When you create an account, SplitFlow stores the name, email address, and securely hashed password needed to identify your account. When you use a room, we store group membership and expense details such as title, amount, category, payer, and date.

02

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, show the rooms you belong to, calculate shared spending, and keep the activity log consistent for your group. We do not use your expense titles or amounts to build advertising profiles.

03

What we share

Room members can see the expense information and member names needed to coordinate shared spending. We do not sell personal information. We only disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

04

Your controls

You can stop using SplitFlow at any time. You can sign out to remove the active session from your browser. To request account information, correction, or deletion, contact the person operating this deployment with the email associated with your account.

05

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcryptjs and sessions use signed JSON Web Tokens. Keep your password private and do not share invite codes outside the intended group. No online service can promise absolute security, so report anything unusual promptly.

06

Policy changes

This policy may change when SplitFlow gains new capabilities. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the revision date shown at the top.
